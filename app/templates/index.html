How Our Weighted Ranking Engine Powers Every Search

Most modern e-commerce storefronts use opaque black-box algorithms or sponsored-only placement that obscure true product merit. Our architecture is built upon a dual-tier microservice model separating the lifestyle consumer storefront from the mathematical ranking engine:

  • Deterministic Min-Max Normalization: Raw metrics across disparate dimensions (battery hours, decibels, latency milliseconds, star ratings) are mapped to a normalized [0.0, 1.0] scale.
  • Personalized Attribute Weights: Category weights enforce a mathematical 100% allocation rule. Shoppers can tune weight sliders to re-rank the catalog dynamically.
  • Strict 1:5 Anti-Bias Sponsored Interleaving: Sponsored campaigns are strictly restricted to 1 slot per 5 organic items (slots 1, 6, 11) with a 20% cap, preserving organic ranking integrity.
  • Resilient Offline Fallback: If the microservice on port 5000 is unavailable or times out, the storefront instantly switches to local SQLite ranking without user disruption.
